---

GradePulse is a teacher dashboard for managing a class roster and quickly identifying students
who need academic support. A teacher can add, update, and delete student records, view live
class statistics (average, passing rate, grade distribution chart), and instantly see a Support
Panel listing every student whose current grade is below the passing threshold — with a clear,
factual reason for each flag. During this hackathon, I explored an unfamiliar app, fixed three
correctness gaps (inconsistent grade thresholds, unreliable save-failure reporting, and
misleading grade labels), and added the Support Panel feature, a grade-distribution chart, and
an 18-student synthetic demo roster. All improvements were made with IBM Bob and are documented
with actual prompts and verified test results in `submission/bob-work-log.md`.
